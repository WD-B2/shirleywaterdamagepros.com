Privacy Policy

What information we collect when you call or visit the site, and how it is used.

☎ (934) 223-1875

This policy explains how Shirley Water Damage Pros handles information when you call (934) 223-1875 or browse shirleywaterdamagepros.com. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Information you give us by phone

When you call, you may tell us your name, address and details about the damage. We use that information to arrange a response and to answer your questions. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information from the website

This site has no forms and does not ask you to type personal details into any page. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic server logs such as page requests, browser type and approximate location. We use that information to keep the site working and secure.

The site embeds Google Maps and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts those services when a page loads. Their own privacy policies apply to that traffic.

How we use and share information

We use call details to arrange service and to improve how the dispatch line works. We share details with the restoration professionals responding to your call so that they can reach you and understand the job. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you or ask us to delete a call record by phoning (934) 223-1875 or emailing contact@shirleywaterdamagepros.com. You can also use your browser settings to block cookies and scripts.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is the current one.
