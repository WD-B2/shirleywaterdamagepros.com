Safety steps before you choose a page

Whatever the cause, a few safety steps come first. Shut off the main water valve if a pipe is the source, keep everyone out of standing water near outlets or the panel, and do not use household fans or heaters on wet walls. Avoid touching anything that could be sewage or flood water, and call 911 or the utility for gas or electrical danger before you call us.

Professional drying and extraction follow the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and mold work follows S520, so ask any company which standards its crews work to.
