Tank failure or loose connection: telling them apart

The source decides how much water you are dealing with and what happens next. The signs below help sort it out before the crew arrives.

Even if the leak looks small, shut off the supply and call. A slow drip under a tank can soak a subfloor for weeks.

When a leaking water heater is an emergency

It is an emergency when water is spreading across the floor, when the tank is hot and hissing, or when the heater sits near an electrical panel, furnace or finished room. A tank that is running while leaking can keep feeding water until the supply is closed.

We take these calls through the night, on weekends and on holidays, and a same-day visit is the normal goal. A heater in a closet or basement corner drains into the places that are hardest to dry, and a long delay can add days of drying. Say on the call whether the unit is gas or electric and whether water is near the panel.

Safety checklist for a leaking water heater

Start with the heater, not the water. Turn off the power to an electric unit at the breaker, or set a gas unit to its off or pilot position, and close the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the tank. Do not touch the heater or its wiring while standing in water.

Water from a tank can be hot enough to scald, so wait before wiping or sweeping it up. Keep children and pets away from the area, and do not try to drain a large leak by hand. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call the dispatch line.

What happens after the heater is shut down

The sequence below keeps hot water, electricity and moisture under control.

  1. Secure the unit

    The crew confirms that water, power and gas supplies are off. A licensed plumber handles the heater itself.

  2. Remove the water

    Pumps, extraction units and a wet vacuum clear the floor, along with carpet and any stored items soaked through.

  3. Trace the spread

    Moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera show how far water wicked into walls and the floor beneath.

  4. Dry the area

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run in the utility space and any room the water reached, following ANSI/IICRC S500.

  5. Document for the claim

    Photos, readings and a written scope go to you, including the water heater label showing its age.

Gas, electric and hot water

Never touch an electric water heater that is standing in water. Do not relight a gas unit that got wet. Hot water can scald, so stay clear of the tank and puddle until the crew arrives.

Where the water ends up and what comes next

Hot water is not sterile, and it can steam into walls. Dry the wall base and check for odor in the days that follow. Mold depends on moisture, the S520 standard guides how it is tested and removed, and a physician handles health questions.

What shapes the cost after a water heater leak

The cost depends on how much water the tank released and which rooms and materials it reached. A heater that failed suddenly is often a covered event, and one that dripped for weeks may be treated as neglect. Check your policy, ask your insurer, and use the photos, readings and written scope the crew provides.
